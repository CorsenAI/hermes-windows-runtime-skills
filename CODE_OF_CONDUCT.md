# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces professional,
technical, and respectful. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment,
personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disruption
are not.

Examples of acceptable participation include:

- focusing criticism on code, documentation, evidence, and trade-offs;
- giving enough context for others to reproduce a result;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting inaccurate claims;
- respecting privacy, disclosure boundaries, and contributor attribution.

Examples of unacceptable participation include:

- targeted insults, intimidation, or repeated unwelcome contact;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- using security reports to pressure, threaten, or publicly expose reporters;
- knowingly submitting malicious code, secrets, or misleading test evidence.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other public or private project communications.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@corsen.ai` with the subject
`[CONDUCT] hermes-windows-runtime-skills`. Include links or records needed to
review the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
or permanently ban a contributor when necessary to protect the project and its
participants. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and
free of retaliation against good-faith reporters.
